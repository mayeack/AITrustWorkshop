An attacker exploits a known vulnerability on a PseudoCo web server. You will watch Splunk Enterprise Security detect it and the AI SOC analyst triage it, plan the response with the Security Assistant, hand the patching to an AI agent through Splunk SOAR under Cisco AI Defense, Cisco Duo and Splunk Agent Observability Controls, approve production on the Change Board, and see Splunk resolve the investigation from the verified fixes.
